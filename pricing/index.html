Questions

How do I subscribe?

In the app, open the menu at the top right, sign in or create an account (a code is emailed to you; there is no password), then choose a plan under Your account. Payment happens on Stripe’s secure page.

Who sells the subscription?

Stripe sells it to you as reseller (merchant of record): it takes the payment, works out sales tax or VAT and sends the receipt. Inqerio never sees your card details.

Can I cancel?

At any time, in the app under Your account, Manage subscription. Your plan continues until the end of the period you paid for. See refunds and cancelling.

What happens to my work if I stop paying?

Nothing is locked or deleted. Your work is in your own folders, and the free parts of the app keep working.
