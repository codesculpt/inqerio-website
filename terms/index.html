The beta

This is a test version of Inqerio. Features may change, be removed or not work as expected. Access codes give testers paid features until the date on the code, free of charge.

Accounts and subscriptions

An account is optional and is for one person; keep access to the email address you sign in with. Subscriptions are paid in advance for a month or a year and renew automatically until you cancel.

Subscriptions are sold through Stripe as reseller (merchant of record), which collects the payment and any sales tax or VAT; Stripe’s terms for buyers also apply. You can cancel at any time under Your account, Manage subscription. Your plan then continues until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are given where the law requires them (including cooling-off periods in some countries), may be given by Stripe within 60 days of a purchase, and are otherwise at our discretion. Prices are shown before you pay, with taxes where they apply.

If a payment fails, the plan keeps working for a short time while Stripe tries again; after that the app returns to the free plan. Your work is never locked: everything you made stays in your folders.

Please keep your own backups of important work. Your files are ordinary files in folders you choose, so your usual backup will include them.
