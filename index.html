What it does

From the first search to the final draft

Each stage of a study in one place, so your evidence, analysis and writing stay connected.

Write

A manuscript editor built for research: tracked changes, comments, versions you can return to, and export to Word.

Cite

Your references and citations in the same place as the writing, in the style your journal asks for.

Find the evidence

Documented, repeatable searches of PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex and more; screening, extraction and the PRISMA 2020 flow diagram.

Analyse

Statistics in your own R, Python, Stata, SAS or SPSS, tied to an analysis plan; thematic, framework and other qualitative approaches.

Prepare ethics

A consultant that reads your protocol and consent form, finds where they disagree and checks them against TCPS 2, CIOMS or your board’s checklist.

AI on your terms

Free AI that runs on your own computer. AI reads only the files you allow, and participant data is never sent without your consent.

Privacy

Your research stays yours
