This policy covers the Inqerio app and its account service. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. Our web host keeps ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, for a short time.

Who we are

Your research stays on your computer. The app does not collect usage data, and nothing you write or analyse is sent anywhere unless you use a feature that needs to, in which case you are told.

Inqerio is made by CodeSculpt, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what the app does with information about you and your research, and it follows Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What stays on your computer

Your projects, documents, references, notes, comments, evidence library, data files, analysis scripts and results, qualitative sources and coding are stored in the folders you choose and in the app’s own folder on your computer. We do not have a copy of them.

The app keeps a few settings on your computer, such as your display name, theme, AI choices, the files that AI may read and your access code if you entered one.

What the app does not do

It does not send usage analytics, tracking data or advertising identifiers. It does not read files outside the project folders you open, except files you choose to import.

When the app connects to the internet

When you run a literature search or look up a DOI, your search terms or the DOI are sent to the databases you chose, for example PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex or Crossref. Their own privacy policies apply.

If you agree during setup, the app downloads Ollama and a model from ollama.com to provide free local AI. After that, the model runs on your computer and your text does not leave it.

The weather on the Home screen is entirely your choice. The app never asks for or detects your location. If you decide to type a city, that name is sent to OpenStreetMap to find its coordinates, and the coordinates are then sent to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) for the forecast, about every 20 minutes while the Home screen is open. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent at all unless you choose a city. The app works fully without one, and you can remove the city from Home at any time.

R and Python are downloaded only when you choose to install them. The app then fetches the official R installer from CRAN or the official Python installer from python.org, checks its signature and installs it for your user only. Packages come from CRAN (R) or PyPI (Python), and a package that a script needs can be installed from the app. Nothing about you or your work is sent.

The app checks for new versions on GitHub, where the installers are published, and downloads them. The check sends the app’s version and your operating system, and nothing about you or your work.

Inqerio AI is available with Pro and Team. Only when you choose it and allow it, the text you send for an AI action goes through our service to the model provider you chose (OpenAI, and later others such as Anthropic or Google, named in the app), solely to answer that request. We do not keep the text or the answers, and the providers do not use them to train their models. We keep only the cost of each request and how many you made each month, so that usage stays within your plan’s allowance. Participant data, such as transcripts and data files, is never sent without your explicit consent for that project.

When you share a project through Inqerio, which requires Team, changes travel between your team’s computers end-to-end encrypted. Our service passes on sealed envelopes that only the receiving computers can open, and it deletes each one as soon as it is collected, or after 30 days. We keep a record of who is on each shared project (email addresses, roles and when they were last seen), each computer’s public key, and invitations (the invited email address, the role and the project’s name) until they are accepted or withdrawn. We cannot read your documents, comments or anything else in the project.

Your account

You can use the app without an account. If you create one, we keep your email address, an account number, and your plan and subscription, meaning which plan, its status and its dates. If you use Inqerio AI or share projects through Inqerio, we also keep what is described above. Your projects, files, notes and analyses are not part of your account and stay on your computers.

Sign-in works with a one-time code sent to your email address. There is no password. The account service runs on Supabase, hosted in Canada. The sign-in emails are sent through our email delivery provider, which receives your address only to deliver them. If you tick “Remember me on this computer”, the app keeps your sign-in on that computer, encrypted by the operating system, for 30 days after you sign out, so that signing in again needs no new code. Untick it on a shared computer, or choose “Forget this computer” on the sign-in form.

For invited testers, we keep the email addresses we have invited and the date each invitation ends, so that the app can open every part for them when they sign in.

Payments are handled by Stripe, which sells the subscription to you as reseller (merchant of record). Stripe receives your name, billing address and card details on its own page, works out sales tax or VAT and keeps payment records as tax law requires. Its own terms and privacy notice apply to the purchase. Your card details never reach us. Stripe tells us whether your subscription is active and when it renews.

We use this information only to sign you in, give you the plan you pay for, and answer your questions. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else.

You can delete your account at any time from the account menu (Your account, then Delete account). This ends any subscription immediately and removes your email address and plan from our service.

Participant and sensitive data

You are responsible for handling research participants’ data as your ethics approval and data agreements require. The app helps by keeping files hidden from AI until you allow them, sending AI only the text you select (or, if you turn on AI review while writing, the paragraphs you change in documents you allowed), and keeping qualitative AI on your own computer by default.

Your choices

You can stop using any online feature, turn AI off for any file, and delete the app and its folder at any time. Because your research is stored on your own computer, deleting it there deletes it entirely.

For questions or requests about your information, contact the publisher at support@inqerio.com.
