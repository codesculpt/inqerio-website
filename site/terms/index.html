Early access

This version of Inqerio is provided to invited testers for evaluation, free of charge and as it is. Features may change, be removed or not work as expected, so keep backups of your work. For an invited tester every part of the app is open until the date of the invitation.

Accounts and subscriptions

An account is optional and is for one person. Keep access to the email address you sign in with. Subscriptions are paid in advance for a month or a year and renew automatically until you cancel.

Subscriptions are sold through Stripe as reseller (merchant of record), which collects the payment and any sales tax or VAT. Stripe’s terms for buyers also apply. You can cancel at any time under Your account, Manage subscription. Your plan then continues until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are given where the law requires them (including cooling-off periods in some countries), may be given by Stripe within 60 days of a purchase, and are otherwise at our discretion. Prices are shown before you pay, with taxes where they apply.

More places on a shared project are a one-time purchase for that project. They stay with the project and count whenever its owner has Team. They are not refunded when the owner’s plan ends, except where the law requires.

If a payment fails, the plan keeps working for a short time while Stripe tries again. After that the app returns to the free plan. Your work is never locked: everything you made stays in your folders.

Please keep your own backups of important work. Your files are ordinary files in folders you choose, so your usual backup will include them.

Using the app

You may install and use the app for your own research, teaching and study. You may not copy, sell, rent or share the app, take its code apart to remove limits, or share access codes with people they were not given to.

You keep all rights to your work. We claim no rights in anything you create with the app.

AI and analysis

AI suggestions and automated checks can be wrong. Statistical, qualitative and ethics guidance in the app is help, not professional advice or approval. You are responsible for your research, its methods and what you publish.

Inqerio AI comes with a monthly allowance that depends on your plan. When it is used up, Inqerio AI pauses until the next month and the free AI on your computer keeps working. Do not send text you are not allowed to share with an AI provider.

Working with others

When you share a project, everyone you invite gets a copy of everything in the project folder (files over 50 MB stay on the computer they are on). Invite only people who may see that material under your ethics approval and data agreements. Roles decide what each person’s changes can alter in the others’ copies. Anyone can still keep what they received.

The owner’s Team plan covers the people they invite (up to five per project): inside that project they can use every part of the app. If the owner’s plan ends, they return to their own plans there, and nothing they made is locked or removed.

Other programs and services

The app works with programs and services we do not control, such as R, Python, Stata, SAS, SPSS, Microsoft Word, Ollama and literature databases. Their own licences and terms apply.
