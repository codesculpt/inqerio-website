Versions of Inqerio

Inqerio keeps itself up to date, so most people never need this page. It lists each released version with what changed.

  • Version 1.0.12

    Latest21 November 2026
    • The project owner can approve sections that teammates changed in a shared document.
    • The strip showing who is in the document appears only when it is needed.

    Download for Windows·Mac, available shortly

Older versions 12

  • Version 1.0.11

    21 November 2026
    • The sharing window is in a clearer order, with less text.

    Download for Windows·Download for Mac

  • Version 1.0.10

    21 November 2026
    • Sharing and invitations are together under Collaborate.
    • Choose whether to invite someone inside Inqerio or by email.

    Download for Windows·Download for Mac

  • Version 1.0.9

    21 November 2026
    • The owner is told when someone’s access to a project, or the project itself, is about to end.
    • People no longer on a project are listed apart from its members.

    Download for Windows·Download for Mac

  • Version 1.0.8

    21 November 2026
    • Work in the same document at the same time as your team: changes appear as they are typed, with each person shown in their own colour.
    • See who is working on a section and who wrote what.
    • Team chat messages are kept for 30 days.

    Download for Windows·Download for Mac

  • Version 1.0.7

    21 November 2026
    • Someone taken off a shared project loses it at once: it closes, cannot be opened, and its copy is removed after 30 days.

    Download for Windows

  • Version 1.0.6

    21 November 2026
    • Team chat has a cleaner, consistent look.
    • Rename a channel or edit your own message with a double click or a right click.

    Download for Windows·Download for Mac

  • Version 1.0.5

    21 November 2026
    • A comment keeps its whole passage when the start or end of that passage is retyped.
    • Comments on passages with citations or line breaks are highlighted and found again.

    Download for Windows·Download for Mac

  • Version 1.0.4

    21 November 2026
    • Teammates’ messages, comments and edits arrive within a second or two.
    • Team chat has channels and mentions with @.

    Download for Windows

  • Version 1.0.3

    21 November 2026
    • Private messages in Team chat, between two people on a shared project.
    • Messages, comments and tags reach teammates within a few seconds.

    Download for Windows·Download for Mac

  • Version 1.0.2

    21 November 2026
    • A project shared through Inqerio reaches a new teammate much faster.
    • The computer that joins shows the files received and the files still to come.
    • A shared project that has arrived is listed on Home straight away.

    Download for Windows·Download for Mac

  • Version 1.0.1

    21 November 2026
    • Inqerio AI is ready for models from Anthropic and Google beside those from OpenAI, with newer models offered as they are released.
    • The privacy policy explains how text sent to Inqerio AI travels to the maker of the model you chose.

    Download for Windows·Download for Mac

  • Version 1.0.0

    21 November 2026
    • The first public version, for Windows and Mac.
    • Systematic reviews, statistics, qualitative analysis and writing in one workspace, with every result traceable to its source.
    • The Inqerio panel in Microsoft Word: citations, results, tables, figures and methods from your project.

    Download for Windows·Download for Mac
