Everything between the question and the paper

One project holds your searches, data, scripts, transcripts and manuscript, so nothing gets lost on the way from one step to the next.

Write with the evidence beside you

Tracked changes, comments and version history, with tables that behave as they do in Word and one-click US, UK or Canadian spelling. Your references, data and notes are one click away.

Cite in 17 styles

References sit beside your text, not in a separate program. Switch from APA to Vancouver, Nature or The Lancet and every citation and the reference list follow.

Search 12 databases at once

PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, OpenAlex, ClinicalTrials.gov and more, from inside the app. Inqerio also writes your strategy out for Ovid, EBSCOhost and Cochrane. Then screen, extract, appraise and draw your PRISMA diagram in the same place.

Analyse in the program you already use

Run R, Python, Stata, SAS, SPSS or a Jupyter notebook, each analysis tied to a plan you wrote first. Not sure where to start? The guided builder writes a commented script you can edit and run anywhere.

See where your answer changes

Forks run one question several defensible ways, side by side. Checks run with your script and each result is explained in plain language, with alternatives ready when a test does not hold.

Go from recording to codebook

Transcribe interviews, code the transcripts and build your codebook as you go. Thematic, framework, content analysis, grounded theory and IPA are all supported, with memos, queries, coder agreement and quotations that carry into your write-up.

Catch ethics gaps early

A consultant reads your protocol and consent form together, points out where they disagree and checks both against TCPS 2, CIOMS or your own board’s checklist.

Work with your team

Share a project with up to five colleagues, end-to-end encrypted. Roles decide who edits, reviews or only reads, and one file sends a search, a review or a library to someone outside the team.

AI that advises and never decides

Free AI runs on your own computer with nothing to set up. It sees only the files you allow, participant data is never sent without your consent, and anything AI touched is labelled.

New in Beta 14

Build models in blocks, keep every script tied to its plan, and get clear answers when a script cannot run.

Build a model, step by step

Add your variables in blocks and fit every model on the same complete cases. Compare them side by side with AIC, likelihood-ratio tests and the change in your main estimate. It covers linear, logistic, count, survival, ordinal and multinomial regression, with the script written in your own R or Python.

Plans and scripts, linked

Select a script and its analysis plan opens beside it. Link scripts to plans from either side, and delete a plan you no longer need. Inqerio asks first if an analysis still uses it.

Checks in plain language

Diagnostics run with your script and each result is explained, with a verdict on whether the test still holds. The reading is advice. The decision stays yours.

A fallback for every test

When a check fails, Inqerio lists the alternatives and when to reach for each, including robust and quantile regression, penalised logistic, zero-inflated counts and stratified Cox.

Errors that explain themselves

Run a colleague’s script and, if a package is missing, Inqerio says whether it is not installed, blocked by Windows or failing to load. Missing packages install in one click.

From transcript to coding

Every transcript shows whether it is a source yet, and one click adds it. Limit recordings to a folder you choose and clear the list when you are done.

R and Python on demand

No statistics program yet? Inqerio installs R or Python for you with the official installer, checked against its digital signature, for your user only and with no administrator rights.

Your research stays yours.

Projects, documents, data and notes are ordinary files in folders you choose. We never have a copy of your work.
