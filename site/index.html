From the first search to the final draft

Every stage of a study in one place, so a search you ran in March is still attached to the result you report in November.

Write

Tracked changes, comments and versions you can return to. Tables behave as they do in Word, spelling moves between US, UK, Canadian and Commonwealth in one click, and the document exports with its tables and figures intact.

Cite

References sit beside the writing rather than in a separate program. Cite in any of 17 styles, from APA and Vancouver to Nature, IEEE and The Lancet. Change the style and every citation and the reference list follow.

Find the evidence

Search twelve databases from inside the app, among them PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, OpenAlex and ClinicalTrials.gov. Inqerio also writes your strategy out for Ovid, EBSCOhost and Cochrane, so the searches you run by hand match the ones it ran. Screening, extraction, appraisal and a PRISMA 2020, PRISMA-ScR or ROSES flow diagram follow in the same place.

Analyse

Statistics in your own R, Python, Stata, SAS or SPSS, or in a Jupyter notebook, with each analysis tied to a plan you wrote first. A guided builder takes you from a data file to a commented script when you want direction, and the script is yours to read, edit and run outside Inqerio.

Test what holds

Forks run the same question several defensible ways at once and show you where the answer changes. Diagnostics run with the script and are read back in plain language, and under every test sit the alternatives for when its assumptions do not hold.

Code the interviews

Transcribe recordings, code the transcripts and build the codebook as you go. Thematic, framework, content analysis, grounded theory and IPA are all at home here, with memos, queries, coder agreement, charts and a write-up that carries your quotations.

Prepare ethics

A consultant reads your protocol and consent form together, points out where the two disagree, and checks both against TCPS 2, CIOMS or your own board’s checklist.

Work together

Share a project with up to five colleagues, end-to-end encrypted. Roles decide who may edit, review or only read, reviewers and coders work in parallel, and a single file sends a search, a review or a library to someone outside the team.

AI on your terms

Free AI runs on your own computer with nothing to set up. It sees only the files you allow, participant data is never sent without your consent, and anything AI touched says so.

New in Beta 9

A guided path from question to verdict, analysis plans that hold you to what you said you would do, and diagnostics that explain themselves.

Build an analysis, guided

From a data file to a running script, one step at a time: your hypothesis worded and approved into the plan, variables in plain roles, every suitable test with the case for choosing it, the standard checks ticked for you, and a commented script in R, Python, Stata, SAS or SPSS that runs outside Inqerio too.

Diagnostics that explain themselves

The checks you tick run with the script and print their results, and each one is read back in plain language with a verdict on whether the test still holds. The reading is advice. The decision stays yours.

A fallback for every scenario

Under each test sit the alternatives for when the diagnostics argue back: robust and quantile regression, penalised logistic, zero-inflated counts, stratified Cox and more, each saying when to reach for it.

Many plans, one working plan

Keep an analysis plan per paper or per analysis. Click one open, mark the working plan your checks and departures follow, and watch a new plan fill itself as you build.

R and Python on demand

No statistics program on the computer? Inqerio installs R or Python for you: the official installer, checked against its digital signature, for your user only, with no administrator rights needed.

Systematic reviews, end to end

Documented searches, screening, appraisal and extraction, PRISMA 2020, PRISMA-ScR or ROSES flow diagrams, study and risk-of-bias tables for your manuscript, and an honest statement of where AI assisted.

Results that read clearly

Fitted models carry readable names, every estimate is explained in plain language with how strong the evidence is and how well the model fits, and results inserted in the manuscript tell you when a rerun changes the numbers.

Your research stays yours.

Projects, documents, data and notes are ordinary files in folders you choose. We never have a copy of your work.
