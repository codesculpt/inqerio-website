Research is divided across too many tools.

The questions that researchers face are growing more complex, yet the tools have not kept pace. A single study may involve a reference manager, a spreadsheet, a statistics package, a transcription service, a word processor and a folder of correspondence. Time is lost moving work between them, and it becomes harder to trace a result in the manuscript back to the data and analysis that produced it.

A single study is spread across a dozen programs.

One workspace for the whole study

Searches, references, data, scripts, interviews and the manuscript are kept in one project. Less time is spent moving work between programs, and more is spent completing it.

Reviews, analysis and writing consume time that is difficult to spare.

Less time on repetitive work

Run a systematic review from one place. The guided analysis builder turns a research question into a commented script. Interviews can be transcribed, and citation styles changed, in a single step. Routine tasks take less time, which leaves more for interpretation.

Too many published results cannot be traced back to the data and analysis behind them.

Reproducible by design

Record the analysis plan before the analysis begins. Every script is plain code that you own and can run outside Inqerio, and results link into the manuscript. Forks test one question in several defensible ways, departures from the plan are recorded, and the version history allows any earlier draft to be restored. A project can be packaged with a checksum for every file, so a reviewer can follow the full trail.

Scientific evidence has never mattered more.

Health, climate and many other global challenges are becoming more complex, and the decisions that address them depend on sound evidence. Researchers need tools that increase their capacity to deliver, so that work is completed faster and more efficiently, and in a form that others can verify and reproduce. That is the purpose of Inqerio.

The tools that support this

Each stage of a study is available within a single project and is connected to the next. Every card names the tab and plan to which the feature belongs.

Editor · Free

Write with the evidence at hand

Track changes, comment and keep a version history, with tables that behave as they do in Word and a choice of US, UK or Canadian spelling. References, data and notes remain one click away.

References · Free

Cite in 17 styles

References sit alongside the text rather than in a separate program. Changing from APA to Vancouver, Nature or The Lancet updates every citation and the reference list. PDFs open within the app, with highlights and comments.

Evidence · Pro

Search 12 databases at once

PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, OpenAlex, ClinicalTrials.gov and others are searched from within the app. Searches can be saved and rerun, and new publications are flagged. Inqerio also writes out your strategy for Ovid, EBSCOhost and Cochrane.

Evidence · Pro

Conduct the review in the same place

Choose from twenty kinds of review, from systematic and scoping to rapid, umbrella and qualitative evidence synthesis, and the steps, forms and reporting guideline follow. Screen titles, abstracts and full texts with reasons and conflicts recorded, extract data, appraise quality and generate a PRISMA 2020, PRISMA-ScR or ROSES diagram, with a record of every step.

Analysis · Pro

Analyse in the software you already use

Run R, Python, Stata, SAS, SPSS or a Jupyter notebook, with each analysis linked to a plan written beforehand. Results link into your manuscript as tables in your journal’s style, and the guided analysis builder produces a commented script that you can edit and run anywhere. Methods run from regression and survival models to factor analysis, structural equation models, meta-analysis, machine learning and Bayesian models.

Analysis · Pro

See where your conclusions change

Forks run one question in several defensible ways, side by side. Checks run with your script, and each result is explained in plain language, with alternatives suggested when an assumption does not hold.

Analysis · Pro

From recording to codebook

Transcribe interviews, code the transcripts and develop the codebook as the work proceeds. Thematic, framework and content analysis, grounded theory and IPA are supported, together with memos, queries, coder agreement and quotations that carry into the manuscript.

Ethics · Pro

Identify ethics issues early

A consultant reads your protocol and consent form together, identifies where they disagree and checks both against TCPS 2, CIOMS or your own board’s checklist.

AI review · Pro

A second reader while you write

In the margin, Inqerio flags claims that need a citation, language stronger than the evidence supports, and figures that disagree. It works only in documents you permit, and only when you switch it on.

Productivity · Free

Monitor your progress

Words written, time spent and writing goals are tracked for a single document or for the whole project. These figures remain on your computer.

Team chat and sharing · Team

Collaborate with your team

Share a project with up to five colleagues, end-to-end encrypted. Roles determine who may edit, review or only read, and a single file can send a search, a review or a library to someone outside the team. Team chat sits beside the work.

AI access · Free

AI that advises and does not decide

The free AI runs on your own computer and is set up in a few clicks when you first open the app. It sees only the files you permit, text is never sent without your consent, and anything AI has touched is labelled.

New in Beta 15

Models built in blocks, scripts linked to their analysis plans, and a clear view of which transcripts are ready to code.

Twenty kinds of review

Choose systematic, scoping, rapid, umbrella, living, qualitative evidence synthesis and more. The type sets the question framework, which steps you need, the extraction form and the reporting guideline.

From extraction to pooled effects

Effect sizes extracted in a review open straight in the guided meta-analysis, ready to pool, with a forest plot and tests of heterogeneity.

More statistical methods

Factor analysis, structural equation models, meta-analysis, mixed models, latent classes, item response theory, clustering, machine learning and Bayesian models, each with a commented script and checks you can tick.

Fitted models you can read

Every fitted method appears under its plan with a plain-language reading, its key figures and what the result does not show. Ask the AI to explain any of them further.

Code for every program

Choose any installed program on a recommendation to see its code in R, Python, Stata, SAS or SPSS, and copy it with the checks you ticked.

Appraisal you can check

Answers suggested by AI can be accepted or rejected in one step. Confirmed answers stay locked until you choose to update them, and a study cannot be marked done until every item is filled in.
