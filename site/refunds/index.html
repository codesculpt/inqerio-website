Refunds and cancelling

Last updated 18 October 2026

You may cancel at any time from the app. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, and your work is never locked.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. In the app, open the menu at the top right, choose Your account, then Manage subscription, and cancel on Stripe’s page. You may also use the link in any receipt email from Stripe.

Your plan continues until the end of the month or year that you paid for, and it is not renewed after that. Nothing you have created is deleted. Your work stays in your own folders, and the free parts of the app continue to work.

Changing plan

A move to a higher plan takes effect at once, and you pay only the difference for the rest of the current period. A move to a lower plan, or from yearly to monthly billing, takes effect at the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

Subscriptions are sold through Stripe as reseller (merchant of record). Refunds are given where the law requires them, including during cooling-off periods in some countries. Stripe may also give a refund within 60 days of a purchase. Otherwise refunds are at our discretion. If something has gone wrong, please write to us and we will review the matter fairly.

To request a refund, email support@inqerio.com with the email address of your account and the date of the payment, or use the support link in your Stripe receipt.

Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, your plan continues to work for a short time while Stripe tries again. After that, the app returns to the free plan until a payment succeeds.
