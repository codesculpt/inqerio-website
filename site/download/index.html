Download Inqerio

Version 1.0.0, November 2026. Free to start, with no account required. The paid plans are bought later, in the app.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Download for Windows

  1. Open the file you downloaded.
  2. Follow the installer. It installs for your own account and needs no administrator password.
  3. Open Inqerio from the Start menu.

Mac

macOS 11 or later. One download for Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Download for Mac

  1. Open the file you downloaded.
  2. Drag Inqerio into the Applications folder.
  3. Open Inqerio from Applications. macOS asks once whether to open an app from the internet.

Good to know

  • Inqerio keeps itself up to date. It tells you when a new version is ready and installs it when you agree.
  • Both versions are signed, and the Mac version is checked by Apple, so your computer can confirm where the app comes from.
  • Your projects are ordinary folders on your computer. Nothing is uploaded when you install or open the app.
  • For statistics, Inqerio works with the R, Python, Stata, SAS or SPSS already on your computer, and can install R or Python for you. SAS is not made for the Mac.

If a download does not start, every version is listed on the releases page. For help, write to support@inqerio.com.
