Download Inqerio

Your reviews, your analysis and your manuscript in one desktop workspace. Install it in a minute and start for free.

  • Free to start
  • No account required
  • Your files stay on your computer

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Download for Windows

Installer (.exe), Version 1.0.14

  1. Open the file you downloaded.
  2. Follow the installer. It installs for your own account and needs no administrator password.
  3. Open Inqerio from the Start menu.

Mac

macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon and Intel

Available shortly

Version 1.0.14 for Mac is being prepared. Please check back in a little while.

  1. Open the file you downloaded.
  2. Drag Inqerio into the Applications folder.
  3. Open Inqerio from Applications. macOS asks once whether to open an app from the internet.

Good to know

It keeps itself up to date

Inqerio tells you when a new version is ready and installs it when you agree.

Signed and checked

Both versions are signed, and the Mac version is checked by Apple, so your computer can confirm where the app comes from.

Private by design

Your projects are ordinary folders on your computer. Nothing is uploaded when you install or open the app.

Works with your statistics software

R, Python, Stata, SAS or SPSS already on your computer, and Inqerio can install R or Python for you. SAS is not made for the Mac.
