Questions

How do I subscribe?

In the app, open the menu at the top right and sign in or create an account (a code is emailed to you, no password needed). Then choose a plan under Your account. Payment happens on Stripe’s secure page.

Who sells the subscription?

Stripe sells it to you as reseller (merchant of record). Stripe takes the payment, including handling the sales tax or VAT and sends you the receipt. Inqerio never sees your card details.

Can I cancel?

At any time, in the app under Your account, Manage subscription. Your plan continues until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens to my work if I stop paying?

Nothing is locked or deleted. Your work is in your own folders, and the free parts of the app keep working.

Do I need an account for the free plan?

No. An account is only needed to subscribe to a paid plan.

Do the people I invite need to pay?

No. They join with a free account, and your Team plan lets them use every part of Inqerio inside the projects you share with them (up to five people per project, and five more places can be added for CA$29, paid once). For projects of their own, their own plan applies.

Can Inqerio read a project I share with my team?

No. Changes travel between your team’s computers end-to-end encrypted: our service passes on sealed envelopes that only the receiving computers can open, and deletes each one once it is collected.

What is the Inqerio AI allowance?

Pro and Team include stronger AI models with a monthly allowance, shown in the app as a percentage used. Transcription comes out of the same allowance, by the minute of audio. When it runs out, Inqerio AI pauses until the next month, and the free AI on your own computer keeps working.

Where does my recording go when it is transcribed?

Only when you press Transcribe, and only after you have allowed AI on that file: the recording is sent in ten-minute pieces to the speech-to-text model you chose, for that one request. The transcript is saved next to the recording on your computer. The service keeps only the cost. Speakers are not identified automatically, so the transcript leaves a blank for each speaker label.
