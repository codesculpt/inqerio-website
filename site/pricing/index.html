Questions

How do I subscribe?

In the app, open the menu at the top right, then sign in or create an account. A code is emailed to you, so no password is needed. Next, choose a plan under Your account. Payment is made on Stripe’s secure page.

Who sells the subscription?

Stripe sells the subscription to you as reseller (merchant of record). Stripe takes the payment, handles any sales tax or VAT and sends you the receipt. Inqerio never sees your card details.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time, in the app under Your account and then Manage subscription. Your plan continues until the end of the period you have paid for.

What happens to my work if I stop paying?

Nothing is locked or deleted. Your work is stored in your own folders, and the free parts of the app continue to work.

Do I need an account for the free plan?

No. An account is needed only to subscribe to a paid plan.

Do the people I invite need to pay?

No. They join with a free account, and your Team plan allows them to use every part of Inqerio inside the projects you share with them. Up to five people can join each project, and five more places can be added for CA$29, paid once. For projects of their own, their own plan applies.

Can Inqerio read a project I share with my team?

No. Changes travel between your team’s computers end-to-end encrypted. Our service passes on sealed envelopes that only the receiving computers can open, and it deletes each one once it has been collected.

What is the Inqerio AI allowance?

Pro and Team include stronger AI models with a monthly allowance, which the app shows as a percentage used. Transcription draws on the same allowance, by the minute of audio. When the allowance is used up, Inqerio AI pauses until the next month, and the free AI on your own computer continues to work.

Where does my recording go when it is transcribed?

The recording leaves your computer only when you select Transcribe, and only after you have allowed AI on that file. It is sent in ten-minute pieces to the speech-to-text model you chose, for that one request, and the transcript is saved next to the recording on your computer. The service keeps only a record of the cost. Speakers are not identified automatically, so the transcript leaves a blank for each speaker label.
